# ZambieLib

The shared library behind ZambieD's mods. If another mod sent you here, just
install this next to it and you are done. It adds no items, no blocks and no
gameplay of its own.

## Mods that use it

- [Wildlore](https://modrinth.com/mod/wildlore): every creature rolls its own
  size, sex and bloodline traits, with real pregnancies and a zoology journal.
- [Broadback](https://modrinth.com/mod/broadback): creatures are solid, stand
  on a cow and ride along as it wanders.
- [DiscordPresence](https://modrinth.com/mod/discordpresence): your Minecraft
  session on Discord, edited by clicking the live card.

## For developers

What lives inside:

- **GuiKit**: vanilla-style panel, inset, scrollbar and progress bar drawing,
  so mod screens look like Mojang built them.
- **JsonStore**: JSON save and load with atomic writes and a `.bak` fallback.
- **Boxes**: bounding box math (volume, overlap, top surfaces).
- **Placeholders**: `%key%` expansion for user-editable text.

Source and issues on
[GitHub](https://github.com/TinsleyDevers/ZambieLib).

## Supported versions

Forge 1.20.1, NeoForge 1.21.1, 1.21.11, 26.1.x and 26.2, and Fabric 26.1.x
and 26.2.
